> Built during my Summer 2023 internship at **Far Eastern Apparel** in Suzhou: a suite of four internal tools that automated the repetitive parts of a 20-person operations team's week. The code is proprietary, so there's no public repo; this page is the spec.

## The problem

An apparel manufacturer's operations team runs on workflows nobody designed: spreadsheets passed around, data re-entered by hand, engagement tracked in whoever's notebook. None of it is hard individually — collectively it's a tax on every week. The internship brief was simple: find the repetitive work and make software do it.

## What I built

- **RPA bots (UiPath)** that took over the most repetitive data-entry workflows — the kind of work where a human adds nothing but error.
- **A React engagement portal** for the team's internal programs, replacing the spreadsheet-and-email loop.
- **An internal Android app** that reached **300 users** inside the company.
- **A Python data-quality pipeline** that caught bad records before they propagated into reports.
- **CI/CD for the suite** — deployments went from a manual checklist to push-button, so the tools could outlive the intern who built them.

## What it taught me

This was the summer that shaped how I think about software: the users were planners, line managers, and HR staff — not engineers. Tools that needed explanation didn't get used; tools that quietly removed a step did. Reliability and obviousness beat cleverness every time.

## Context

Built as a software / IT intern, July–September 2023, Suzhou. The tools are internal to Far Eastern Apparel, so the source is not public — the [experience timeline](/#experience) and my resume carry the verifiable details.
